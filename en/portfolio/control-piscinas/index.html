The challenge

Operations management
with no central system

The sports facilities managed monthly memberships, the store and stock manually, with no real-time visibility into the business. It was impossible to catch customer retention problems or profitability deviations in time.

The solution

Mobile ERP with
business intelligence

A full ERP application was built in AppSheet on top of Google Sheets, covering billing, store, inventory, customers and suppliers. As a data-driven complement, a Looker Studio dashboard was built so management could visualize operations and make data-based decisions.
