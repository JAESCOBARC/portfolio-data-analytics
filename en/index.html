Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask me

How much does a Power BI or Tableau dashboard cost?

Business Intelligence dashboards start from €500 for basic solutions connected to a single data source. Projects with multiple sources, custom KPIs and training included range between €800 and €2,500, depending on complexity.

How long does a data analytics project take to develop?

A standard dashboard is delivered in 1-2 weeks. Automation projects or full AppSheet applications can take between 2 and 6 weeks, depending on complexity and data availability.

Do you work with companies of any size?

Yes. I work with small businesses and freelancers as well as departments of large companies. Solutions are adapted to the budget and real needs of each business.

What tools do you use for data analysis?

I mainly work with Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, Python (pandas, matplotlib), SQL, Excel with VBA, AppSheet and n8n for automation. The tool is chosen based on the client's context and data.

Do you offer Excel or Power BI training for teams?

Yes. I offer corporate training in Excel, Power BI and data analysis for teams. Programs are tailored to the team's level and needs.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

I work mainly remotely, which lets me collaborate with companies across Spain and internationally. For projects in Granada and Andalusia, I can also work on-site if required.
